# Welcome to Mizzenmast {#welcome}
In the age of sail, the mizzenmast wasn’t the biggest or flashiest mast on a ship — but it was the one that kept you steady, gave you control, and helped you maneuver through changing winds. It was the difference between drifting aimlessly and charting your own course.

At Mizzenmast, we aim to play that same role for your games. Whether it’s supplements that expand your RPG adventures, gear and scenarios for epic Nerf Wars, or tools that make life easier (and more fun) for players and GMs, we’re here to give you the control, creativity, and momentum to steer your adventures wherever you want them to go.

We’re not the whole ship — we’re the part that helps you sail it better.

<div class="banner under-construction">
<h2>Early Days Yet</h2>
</div>

We’re still rigging the sails and stowing the cargo. Mizzenmast is in the early days of its voyage, which means our site and product lines are still growing. You might find a few empty shelves in the hold right now, but we’re hard at work filling them with adventures, tools, and experiences worth the journey.

As we chart new courses — from fresh RPG supplements to bigger Nerf War events and creative tools for GMs and players — we invite you to climb aboard early. The wind’s picking up, and we can’t wait to see where this ship will take us.
