<h1 id="welcome" class="ds-gradient-header">Welcome to Quantum Arc</h1>

Quantum Arc is an expansion for *DRAW STEEL* designed to power cinematic, tactical, heroic, and science fantasy adventures. The name Quantum Arc reflects its purpose: to serve as a bridge across countless settings, from iconic universes like Mass Effect, Anthem, and Destiny, to entirely original worlds of your own creation.

Fully compatible with the core rules of MCDM’s Draw Steel, Quantum Arc extends the system with modular content that’s easy to adapt and expand. New classes, ancestries, rules, monsters, and more are in development, giving you a growing collection of tools to create high-tech legends and far-future fables however tickles your fancy.

Quantum Arc is more than a rule set—it's a community-driven project. We believe that the best ideas come from collaboration, creativity, and open dialogue. Whether you’re a Director homebrewing your own setting, trying to recreate elements of your favorite science fantasy setting, or a player dreaming up new sci-fi heroes, your voice matters here. We welcome feedback, homebrew contributions, and creative expansions from the community to help shape the future of Quantum Arc together. While not every idea will make it into Quantum Arc, we listen intently and will implement many, if not most of your contributions!

Perhaps most importantly: Quantum Arc will always be free to access online. The digital rules and content are available to everyone, forever—because this galaxy belongs to all of us.

## Multiverse by Design {#multiverse-by-design}
A multiplicity of worlds is at the heart of Quantum Arc’s philosophy. Rather than anchoring gameplay to a single setting, Quantum Arc exists in the state between your specific setting and no setting at all. It is a modular framework that empowers Directors and players to jump between vastly different science fantasy worlds or to craft their own using a set of useful tools. Whether you're running military space opera, mythic techno-fantasy, or something else entirely, Quantum Arc will give you the tools to make it all feel cohesive, playable, and epic—by design, not accident.

## New Classes {#new-classes}
New Classes in Quantum Arc are designed to bring science fantasy archetypes to life. Each class is built for tactical depth and cinematic flair, while staying fully compatible with Draw Steel’s core mechanics. And we’re just getting started! New classes are in active development, and community-created classes are not only welcome, but encouraged. If you’ve got a vision for a spacefaring hero or high-tech warrior, we want to help you bring it into Quantum Arc.

## New Ancestries {#new-ancestries}
New Ancestries in Quantum Arc let you embody the diverse and exotic beings that populate science fantasy worlds. You'll be able to experience play as gene-modded humans, synthetic lifeforms, and a variety of alien species with strange powers and rich cultures. Each ancestry is designed to offer unique mechanical options and story hooks that fit seamlessly into a wide range of settings. Just like everything in Quantum Arc, community-created ancestries are highly encouraged. Whether you're adapting favorites from your favorite media or inventing something entirely new, there's a place for your creations in the ever-expanding multiverse.

## New Monsters {#new-monsters}
New Monsters in Quantum Arc bring the threats and wonders of science fantasy to your table. From nanite swarms, ancient war machines, alien scavengers and mercenaries, and even extradimensional horrors can make an appearance. Each creature is built to challenge players both tactically and narratively, fitting into a wide variety of settings. As with all aspects of Quantum Arc, community contributions are welcome and encouraged. Your creations can help populate the multiverse with danger, mystery, and awe.

## New Rules {#new-rules}
New Rules in Quantum Arc provide the flexibility to tailor your games to the unique needs of your science fantasy setting. Whether it’s advanced tech systems, low or zero-gravity combat, or cover options, the rules we present on Quantum Arc expand on Draw Steel’s core framework to bring your vision to life. Just as with classes, ancestries, and monsters, community-created rules are encouraged. If you’ve developed a mechanism or system that enhances the experience, we’d love to help integrate it into the growing toolkit within Quantum Arc.

## Community Driven {#community-driven}
When we say that Quantum Arc is community driven, it is more than a tagline. It’s a core principle. This project thrives on the creativity, passion, and collaboration of the people who are inspired by it. We believe the best content comes from a shared love of storytelling, and we actively welcome contributions from the community, including classes, ancestries, monsters, rules, settings, art, or even just feedback on our current designs. Quantum Arc is a living project, shaped by its players, and we're committed to building an open, inclusive space where your ideas can take flight and become part of the multiverse.

## Free Forever {#free-forever}
Free Forever is a foundational promise of Quantum Arc. The digital rules, content, and updates on this site will always be freely available to everyone. We believe that great gameplay should be accessible to all. This means that you can dive into the rules, explore new content, and contribute to the growing universe without barriers. While future physical content, more fully featured applications, or other tools may not be free, as long as Quantum Arc exists it will have a free version here!

## Overall Changes for Quantum Arc
We've made a few overall changes which apply broadly. Keep these basic changes in mind as you read and review any other Quantum Arc content.

### Magic → Tech <span class="badge rounded-pill text-bg-success align-top status-pill">CHANGE</span>
Clarke’s third law states that “Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic.” Many of our classes or abilities use technology rather than magic. In those cases, we’ve replaced the term Magic with Tech. It fills the same niche, but fits our desired tone a bit better. Any effect that would impact the Magic keyword will work with the Tech keyword, and vice versa.

### Psionic → Gravitic <span class="badge rounded-pill text-bg-success align-top status-pill">CHANGE</span>
“What’s the difference between psionics and gravitics?” you ask.

There isn’t one, really. <mark class="editorial-highlight">We just wanted to make up our own quasi-scientific term for abilities that allow people to do crazy stuff like telepathy and telekinesis.</mark> Any effect that would impact the Psionic keyword will work with the Gravitic keyword, and vice vera.

### Classes Without Kits <span class="badge rounded-pill text-bg-success align-top status-pill">CHANGE</span>
Quantum Arc assumes that technology has advanced to the point that all characters are capable of using Light Ranged Weapons (such as pistols and submachine guns) and wearing light armor without specialized training. Classes that do not gain kits still do not gain access to any of the bonuses or Signature Actions granted by kits unless they obtain access to a kit through a class option.

Any abilities, enhancements, or features that grant access to light weapons and light armor, for equipment or treasures, instead allow characters to use medium weapons or armor.
